A lone traveler standing atop a windswept cliff edge, back to the viewer,
gazing over a vast unexplored fantasy landscape — floating islands, distant

discovery and an open horizon waiting to be explored.

recognizable copyrighted characters — symbolic and atmospheric rather than a
