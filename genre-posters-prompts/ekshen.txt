Жанр: Экшен

A dynamic mid-air clash: two silhouetted fighters colliding sword against
sword, sparks exploding outward, a shockwave rippling through drifting dust
and debris, motion-blurred limbs and cape. Dramatic low-angle composition,
high-contrast lighting, saturated red-and-blue palette, pure adrenaline and
kinetic energy frozen at the peak of impact.

Cinematic anime-style digital painting, dramatic lighting, rich color
palette, highly detailed, poster key-art composition, vertical portrait
orientation (aspect ratio 2:3). No text, no logos, no watermarks, no
recognizable copyrighted characters — symbolic and atmospheric rather than a
specific character portrait.
