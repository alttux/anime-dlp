Жанр: Военное

A war-torn battlefield at dusk: silhouettes of soldiers advancing through
drifting smoke and ruined buildings, tattered military banners caught in the
wind, distant fighter planes streaking overhead, scattered embers and debris
in the air. Muted khaki, rust-orange and ash-grey palette with a single
smoldering red glow on the horizon. Sense of tension, sacrifice and quiet
gravity.

Cinematic anime-style digital painting, dramatic lighting, rich color
palette, highly detailed, poster key-art composition, vertical portrait
orientation (aspect ratio 2:3). No text, no logos, no watermarks, no
recognizable copyrighted characters — symbolic and atmospheric rather than a
specific character portrait.
