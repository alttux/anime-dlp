Жанр: Фантастика

A sprawling futuristic cityscape at night, towering neon-lit skyscrapers,
flying vehicles weaving between buildings, a lone figure in a sleek
high-tech suit standing on a ledge gazing at a massive glowing holographic
display. Cool cyan-and-magenta cyberpunk palette, sense of vast technological
scale and possibility.

Cinematic anime-style digital painting, dramatic lighting, rich color
palette, highly detailed, poster key-art composition, vertical portrait
orientation (aspect ratio 2:3). No text, no logos, no watermarks, no
recognizable copyrighted characters — symbolic and atmospheric rather than a
specific character portrait.
