Жанр: Психологическое

A fractured mirror reflecting a distorted, fragmented silhouette of a person,
shards floating apart from the frame, surreal shifting geometric shapes
bleeding into the background reality. Cold deep-blue and violet tones, high
contrast, unsettling and introspective mood that suggests a fractured mind
rather than any physical threat.

Cinematic anime-style digital painting, dramatic lighting, rich color
palette, highly detailed, poster key-art composition, vertical portrait
orientation (aspect ratio 2:3). No text, no logos, no watermarks, no
recognizable copyrighted characters — symbolic and atmospheric rather than a
specific character portrait.
