Жанр: Триллер

A shadowy figure lurking at the far end of a rain-slicked alley, lit only by
a single flickering neon sign reflected in wet pavement, deep pooling
shadows on either side. Tense film-noir atmosphere, desaturated palette with
one sharp accent color, a strong sense of imminent, unseen danger just
outside the frame.

Cinematic anime-style digital painting, dramatic lighting, rich color
palette, highly detailed, poster key-art composition, vertical portrait
orientation (aspect ratio 2:3). No text, no logos, no watermarks, no
recognizable copyrighted characters — symbolic and atmospheric rather than a
specific character portrait.
